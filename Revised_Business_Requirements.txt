================================================================================
             HALLO BARBER SHOP - SYSTEM BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS (BR)
                    (Version: 100% Aligned with Current Code)
================================================================================

This document compiles all Business Rules (BR) currently active in the Hallo BarberShop codebase. Pending or unimplemented features from legacy designs have been completely omitted to maintain specification integrity.

---

================================================================================
PART 1: LIST OF ACTIVE SYSTEM BUSINESS RULES (BR-001 TO BR-074)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 1: AUTHENTICATION & AUTHORIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-001: New users can register a membership account with their Full Name, Email, Phone Number, and Password.
- BR-002: Newly created accounts default to the customer role and remain unverified.
- BR-003: Users registering with Email/Password must verify their account via a one-time password (OTP) sent to their email before logging in for the first time. Accounts signing in via Google are exempt from email OTP verification.
- BR-004: User sessions are managed via secure authentication tokens stored in the browser to control access to protected system features.
- BR-005: Users can log in using either their Email/Password credentials or via Google Quick Login.
- BR-006: If a Google email address already exists as a standard email/password account in the system, the system automatically links the Google account to the existing profile and marks it as verified.
- BR-007: Users can request to resend the verification OTP if the previous code has expired.
- BR-008: Users can request a password reset via a confirmation link sent to their email.
- BR-009: Each customer can only view and update their own personal profile. Only administrators have the authority to suspend/reactivate, change roles, or modify account information for other users.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 2: SERVICE & BARBER MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-010: Both registered customers and guest customers are permitted to book appointments online. There is no limit on the number of active bookings a customer may hold.
- BR-011: Guest customers booking online must provide their Name and Contact Phone Number; providing an Email address is not mandatory.
- BR-012: The system automatically checks for schedule conflicts, ensuring the requested appointment time slot does not overlap with any existing bookings for the selected barber.
- BR-013: The system strictly blocks appointment bookings on days when a barber is marked off-duty or has an approved leave request.
- BR-014: Each barber has a designated maximum threshold of appointments allowed per working day (defaulting to 12 bookings per day) to maintain service quality.
- BR-015: Users are prohibited from booking appointments for times and dates that have already passed.
- BR-016: If a customer does not specify a barber when booking (or when booking through the chatbot), the system automatically assigns an available barber who is free during that time slot.
- BR-017: When an administrator creates a new barber account, the system automatically initializes their barber profile and generates a default 14-day schedule template to make them instantly bookable online.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3: BOOKING LIFECYCLE & OPERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-018: Appointments follow a strict status lifecycle: Pending -> Confirmed -> Completed, or Cancelled / Rejected / No-Show.
- BR-019: Administrators and reception staff have full authority to approve, reject, bulk-approve, or reassign barbers for any appointment.
- BR-020: When an appointment is cancelled or rejected, the system immediately releases the reserved time slots on the barber's schedule, making them available for other customers.
- BR-021: The system provides a public interface for customers to check the remaining open time slots for any barber on a given day.
- BR-022: Customers can only view, modify, or request the cancellation of bookings they own.
- BR-023: The system automatically sends email updates and in-app notifications to customers whenever their assigned barber or booking details are changed.
- BR-024: The system automatically triggers in-app notifications to customers when their appointment is cancelled, rejected, or reassigned to a different barber by staff.
- BR-025: Administrators can view all pending appointments and filter them by Date, Barber, and Service.
- BR-026: Administrators can reject pending bookings, and the system requires them to input a rejection reason and operational note.
- BR-027: All detailed descriptions, such as cancellation reasons, no-show notes, reschedule reasons, or additional remarks, are permanently stored alongside the booking record.
- BR-028: Reception staff or authorized managers can create manual bookings directly in the system on behalf of walk-in or phone-in customers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 4: QUALITY & PRICING CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-029: For appointments containing multiple services, the total cumulative duration and calculated price must equal the mathematical sum of each individual service item.
- BR-030: The system prevents customers from selecting barbers who are already fully booked or unavailable during the requested time slot.
- BR-031: Pricing, service categories, and duration details must be transparently displayed for customers to search and review.
- BR-032: If a customer requests a service modification that increases the total appointment duration, the change is permitted only if the extended time frame is free.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 5: PAYMENTS & VOUCHERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-033: The minimum online booking lead time is 30 minutes before the scheduled start time.
- BR-034: Vouchers applied to an appointment are temporarily locked for 15 minutes during the checkout process to wait for the online payment transaction status.
- BR-035: Prepayment and deposit rules:
  - Customers with 1 no-show infraction: Required to pay a 50% deposit online via the payment gateway.
  - Customers with 2 no-show infractions: Required to make a 100% full prepayment online via the payment gateway.
  - Customers with 0 infractions: Exempt from online prepayments (allowed to pay in-store after the service).
- BR-036: Bookings made by customers with a clean history (0 infractions) are confirmed and held on the schedule without requiring prepayment.
- BR-037: Customers cannot cancel or reschedule appointments within 2 hours of the scheduled start time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 6: LEAVE & ROSTER MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-038: Barbers can submit leave requests by selecting a start date, end date, and providing a reason.
- BR-039: Leave requests follow a standard status lifecycle: Pending -> Approved or Rejected.
- BR-040: Once a leave request is approved, the system automatically blocks the barber's schedule for that period, preventing any new customer bookings.
- BR-041: The public booking calendar automatically hides availability and time slots for barbers during their approved leave periods.
- BR-042: When approving a barber's leave, the system displays overlapping customer bookings. Administrators must resolve these manually by reassigning them to other barbers or cancelling and notifying the customers.
- BR-043: When reviewing leave requests, the system cross-references active bookings and opens a conflict resolution screen for the administrator to handle reassignments or cancellations.
- BR-044: The system supports weekly roster scheduling, allowing staff to register for shifts (morning/afternoon) based on the staffing capacity template set by administrators before publishing the final roster.
- BR-045: The system runs automated background processes weekly: generating a blank roster template on Thursdays, and publishing the finalized roster on Sundays.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 7: NO-SHOW & PENALTY POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-046: If a customer cancels an appointment late (less than 2 hours before the start time) or fails to show up, the system automatically records a violation infraction under their profile.
- BR-047: Administrators and reception staff have the authority to update an appointment status to "No-Show" directly from the dashboard.
- BR-048: Appointments marked as "No-Show" forfeit any online deposits or prepayments, and the customer's violation count increments by one.
- BR-049: Staff or barbers can mark a customer as no-show at any time after they miss their scheduled start time.
- BR-050: Customers are automatically blocked from making new online bookings if they accumulate three (3) or more active non-excused infractions. The system counts total cumulative infractions and does not automatically reset.
- BR-051: Once the infraction threshold is breached (3 or more), the customer profile is blocked indefinitely until reviewed and manually unblocked by an administrator.
- BR-052: Guest customers (unregistered) who repeatedly violate policies are identified and blocked from online booking based on their phone number.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 8: ADMIN OPERATIONS & POS BILLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-053: In-store checkout supports a unified POS billing module, allowing staff to add retail products to service invoices and complete payments in a single transaction via cash or online payment gateway transfers.
- BR-054: The system aggregates business analytics reporting total revenue, completed booking counts, and new customer sign-ups over day, month, and year periods.
- BR-055: The system reports top-performing barbers ranked by the actual revenue generated from their completed appointments.
- BR-056: The system displays interactive trend charts showing booking counts over time.
- BR-057: Operational dashboards track key performance indicators such as cancellation rates, no-show rates, net revenue, and workload distribution among barbers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 9: AI CHATBOT ASSISTANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-058: The AI Chatbot is empowered with tool-calling integrations, allowing it to directly mutate system state by creating bookings, rescheduling dates, cancelling appointments, or placing retail orders without manual staff intervention.
- BR-059: The AI Chatbot adheres to pre-configured guidelines and response scopes (restricting responses to shop services, products, barbers, and bookings, and avoiding financial or legal statements).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 10: E-COMMERCE & REVIEWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BR-060: Guest customers can purchase retail products online without needing to register a membership account.
- BR-061: Each user session (registered or guest) is restricted to a maximum of one active shopping cart at any time.
- BR-062: Vouchers are applied successfully only when they are within their validity period, target the correct items (services/products), and the order meets the minimum purchase value requirement.
- BR-063: For percentage-based vouchers, the discount amount is capped at the maximum discount value defined in the voucher configuration.
- BR-064: Product prices are locked at the time of order creation; subsequent changes to catalog prices do not affect completed or pending orders.
- BR-065: Guest customers must provide a valid Name and contact Phone Number at checkout to complete a product order or book an appointment.
- BR-066: Product orders track payment and fulfillment statuses: Pending, Paid, or Refunded.
- BR-067: The system automatically deducts product inventory quantities immediately upon a successful order transaction.
- BR-068: Customers are only permitted to review services and barbers after the corresponding appointment status is marked as Completed.
- BR-069: Each appointment is limited to exactly one service feedback submission.
- BR-070: Customer feedback comprises a star rating (1 to 5 stars) and a free-text review comment.
- BR-071: Barber and product aggregate ratings are automatically updated and displayed publicly immediately after feedback is submitted.
- BR-072: When a registered member submits a feedback review, the system automatically awards them 50 loyalty points and generates a 10% gift voucher.
- BR-073: Guest customers can review retail products by submitting their valid transaction order code for verification.
- BR-074: The system maintains an in-app notification service to alert customers of events such as voucher rewards, refund updates, and schedule changes.
